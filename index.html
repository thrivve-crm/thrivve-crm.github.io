Call quality sample

3 random answered calls per agent, each with at least 30 seconds of talk time. Once drawn, the sample for a day never changes. Unreviewed calls stay pending and do not count as zero.

Review list

Separate from the four measures. Each item shows the rule and the evidence. Nothing here deducts points automatically — the reviewer decides.

Follow-up adherence

A follow-up is on time if its owner made a Maqsam call or logged a call/WhatsApp from the due date through the next 2 working days, even if the customer did not answer. Days off and holidays are skipped. Pending and awaiting-data follow-ups are not counted.
